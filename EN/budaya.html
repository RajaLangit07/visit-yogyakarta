Culture

Discover Yogyakarta's rich cultural wealth of traditions and heritage.

Keraton Culture & Customary Traditions

The heart of Jogja's culture, which springs from the Ngayogyakarta Hadiningrat Keraton. This culture includes various sacred ceremonies and folk traditions that are still preserved today, such as Grebeg (the scramble for the gunungan of harvest offerings), Sekaten, and Labuhan.

Classical Wayang Performing Arts

A heritage of traditional Javanese performing arts that blends philosophical stories, music, and movement. Prime examples are Wayang performances (leather puppet and human), the Ramayana Ballet at Prambanan, and the sound of Gamelan that accompanies many customary events.

Traditional Craft Arts

Jogja's signature handicrafts, renowned worldwide and of high aesthetic value. This identity is represented by Jogja Hand-Drawn Batik with its sogan colors and philosophical motifs, as well as Kotagede Silver Craft with its detailed engravings.
