Tourism

Three destinations that must make your Yogyakarta travel list.

Malioboro Street

The bustling heart of Yogyakarta, full of batik shops, handicrafts, and street food. Best enjoyed on a leisurely stroll, especially at night.

Prambanan Temple

A magnificent 9th-century Hindu temple complex with tall, soaring towers. One of the most beautiful architectural legacies in Indonesia.

Parangtritis Beach

An iconic beach in southern Yogyakarta with wide stretches of sand, big waves, and stunning sunsets.
